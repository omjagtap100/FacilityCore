# FacilityCore — society committee, Android

Create 18 separate Android screens, A1 through A18. Each section below is a new screen. Do not edit, replace, restyle, or overwrite a screen you already made. Keep all 18.

## Master

These screens are the FacilityCore app for the society committee: secretary, treasurer, and facility manager. A resident never sees them.

Every screen is its own Android phone screenshot, portrait, 390 by 844, with the phone status bar. Do not draw a website, a desktop page, a left sidebar, or a wide data table. Do not turn one screen into the next.

Look: warm off-white #F7F6F3, ink text #1C1B19, secondary text #6F6A63, 1px lines #E4E0D8. One plain sans-serif. 24px side padding. No shadow, gradient, illustration, emoji, or icon grid. One filled button, black #1C1B19, label #F7F6F3, 48px tall, full width, 8px corners. Any other action is plain text. Red #9C3B2E only for overdue or a rejected payment. Green #1F6B45 only when money is confirmed.

Chrome on every committee screen: top title, under it the small grey line “Palm Court CHS”. Content is a vertical list or a short form. Rows are full width with a hairline under each row. Nothing sits side by side.

Sample flat B-1204, Anita Kulkarni, March due ₹4,850. English labels. Do not add vendors, a services shop, visitors, charts, or chat.

A1 through A18 are eighteen different screens. Add each one. Leave the others as they are.
