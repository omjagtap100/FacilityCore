How to read this

Product Phase 1 is the whole core app. Inside it, nine steps follow the BRD. BRD phase 6 (automatic WhatsApp), priced services, and the gate sit after this phase. Local vendors do not: no phonebook and no 2 km directory, in these nine steps or after them. A mark on the chart means “build this feature in this step”. Finish a step and pass its check before starting the next. Hindi and Marathi cover every screen as that screen is built. The language choice itself is step 1.
